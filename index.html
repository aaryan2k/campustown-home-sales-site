Medians are computed in your browser from the CSV file each time the page loads. The median is the middle sale price when a city's sales are sorted from lowest to highest; with an even number of sales, it is the average of the two middle prices. The data description sheet gives medians that leave out sales under $1,000, so its figures will differ slightly.

Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
